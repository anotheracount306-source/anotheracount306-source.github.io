Estudio Creador Hub

EN · Estudio Creador Hub is a private tool that a single creator runs on their own computer to schedule and upload their own videos to their own social accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram). On TikTok it only sends finished videos to the creator's drafts (inbox) through the official Content Posting API; the creator reviews and publishes each one from the TikTok app. It is not offered to the public and does not manage anyone else's accounts.

ES · Estudio Creador Hub es una herramienta privada que un creador usa en su propia computadora para programar y subir sus propios videos a sus propias cuentas. En TikTok solo manda los videos terminados a los borradores del creador con la API oficial; el creador los revisa y publica desde la app.
